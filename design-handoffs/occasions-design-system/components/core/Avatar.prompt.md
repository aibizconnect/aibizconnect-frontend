**Avatar** — circular user image or initials (initials render on the brand gradient).

```jsx
<Avatar name="Dana Ruiz" size="md" status="online" />
<Avatar src="/clients/acme.png" name="Acme LLC" size="lg" />
```

- Sizes `xs` 24 · `sm` 32 · `md` 40 · `lg` 52 · `xl` 72.
- `status` adds an online/away/offline presence dot. Use in contact lists, comment threads, and the app header.
