### 1. Services — what's included

For the Setup Fee and Monthly Fee in Section 4, Provider will:

- Provision **one (1)** business account from Provider's standard LeadLoop
  system template.
- Connect up to **three (3)** lead sources (e.g., website form, listing/lead
  portal, Facebook/Instagram lead ads, missed-call text-back).
- Connect **one (1)** calendar for appointment booking.
- Load and tune Provider's industry message library to Client's business name,
  sender voice, and brand, including **one (1) round of copy revisions**.
- Submit the required carrier (A2P 10DLC) registration under **Client's own**
  business identity (see Section 6) and run a go-live quality check.
- Provide **30 minutes** of training/handoff for Client's team.
- Provide **ongoing light account management** and a **monthly summary report**
  of booked appointments, for as long as the Monthly Fee is active.

### 2. Out of scope — change orders

LeadLoop is a proven system configured to Client's business — **not** a
custom software build. The following are **not included** in the Setup Fee or
Monthly Fee and will be quoted separately, in writing, before any work begins
("**Change Order**"):

- Building or redesigning Client's website.
- Custom software, custom features, or bespoke integrations beyond Provider's
  standard connectors.
- Copy rewrites beyond the one included revision round.
- Managing or optimizing paid advertising (Meta, Google, or otherwise).
- Migrating unrelated data (legacy CRMs, spreadsheets not tied to lead flow).
- Additional lead sources beyond three, additional calendars, new workflows,
  or new business verticals.

### 3. Turnaround & responsiveness

- Provider targets go-live **approximately 2–5 business days** after Client
  completes intake, subject to third-party carrier (A2P 10DLC) approval
  timelines, which are outside Provider's control.
- Client agrees to respond to Provider's requests (calendar access, assets,
  approvals) within **approximately two (2) business days**. If Client does
  not, timelines extend accordingly and Provider is not responsible for the
  resulting delay.

### 4. Fees & billing

- **Setup Fee:** **$497 USD**, one-time, due at enrollment. Once Provider
  begins the build, the Setup Fee is **non-refundable**.
- **Monthly Fee:** **$197 USD/month**, billed via Stripe on a recurring,
  month-to-month, auto-renewing basis beginning at go-live (or per Section
  4(c) below).
- **Cancellation:** Client may cancel the Monthly Fee at any time with **[X]
  days'** written notice to support@aibizconnect.ca, effective at the end of
  the then-current billing period. No refunds are issued for partial months.
- **14-Day Guarantee (service credit):** if LeadLoop has not produced at least
  one booked appointment within the first 14 days of go-live, Provider will
  waive Client's next Monthly Fee as a service credit. This guarantee is
  **contingent on Client actually supplying or receiving qualifying leads**
  during that period (via connected lead sources) and on Client responding to
  Provider's requests per Section 3; it is a service credit, not a cash refund,
  and not a guarantee of business outcomes, revenue, or lead volume (see
  Section 8).
- **Optional Reactivation Sprint:** an optional one-time service (typically
  $300–$500, quoted at the time) to run outreach against Client's existing
  contact list, subject to Section 6(d) below.

### 5. Client responsibilities

Client agrees to:

- Provide accurate, complete business information (legal business name,
  address, taxpayer/registration number, website) for carrier registration.
- Connect a working calendar for booking.
- Supply only contact lists Client is authorized to message under Section 6.
- Respond to Provider's requests within the timeframe in Section 3.
- Obtain Client's own legal, compliance, or licensing sign-off where required,
  including under Section 9 for regulated fields.

### 6. SMS compliance & consent

This section is the core of the relationship and is not negotiable without
counsel review.

**(a) Consent warranty.** Client represents and warrants that **every contact
loaded into LeadLoop has given prior express consent** to be contacted by SMS
and/or email by Client's business (e.g., by submitting an inquiry, a form, or
otherwise initiating contact), and that Client has records supporting that
consent on request.

**(b) Sender of record.** Messages sent through LeadLoop are sent under
**Client's own** carrier registration (A2P 10DLC "Brand" and "Campaign"),
Client's own business identity, and Client's own phone number. **Client is the
sender and originator of all messages.** Provider is solely the technology and
setup provider that configures the system on Client's behalf; Provider is not
a party to, and assumes no responsibility for, the content or recipients of
any message sent under Client's registration.

**(c) Client's compliance responsibility.** Client is solely responsible for
compliance with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), Canada's
Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), and all other applicable messaging, marketing,
telemarketing, and data-privacy laws in the jurisdictions where Client
operates or messages recipients, and for the content of every message sent
under Client's account.

**(d) Opt-out and list hygiene.** STOP/opt-out handling and quiet-hours
enforcement are enabled by default and may not be disabled. Client will not
load, and Provider will not knowingly send to, cold or purchased contact
lists. Reactivation messaging (Section 4) against an older or previously
inactive contact list will only be run **after Client provides a written
attestation that the list consists of contacts who previously consented to
being contacted**, and, for regulated Clients, after the additional review in
Section 9.

Client agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Provider, its officers,
employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, damages, losses,

### 8. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law: (a) Provider's total liability under
this Agreement, for any claim of any kind, is capped at the total fees Client
paid to Provider in the **three (3) months** preceding the claim; (b) Provider
makes no guarantee of lead volume, appointment volume, revenue, or any other
business outcome — the 14-Day Guarantee in Section 4 is the sole and exclusive
remedy for underperformance; and (c) Provider is not liable for message
deliverability, carrier approval timing or rejection, or other factors
controlled by third-party carriers, telecom providers, or platform vendors
outside Provider's reasonable control.

### 9. Regulated-segment addendum

This section applies **in addition to** the rest of this Agreement whenever
Client operates in a licensed or regulated field (including, without
limitation, law, insurance, health, or financial services):

- Client's **licensed principal, compliance officer, or (for law firms) bar
  counsel** must review and approve all message copy **before go-live**.
  Provider will not activate SMS sending for a regulated Client until this
  sign-off is received.
- Client is solely responsible for compliance with all advertising,
  solicitation, and disclosure rules applicable to its profession (e.g.,
  attorney-advertising disclaimers, insurance-licensing disclosures, no
  quoting of rates or guaranteed outcomes), and for including any required
  disclaimers in message copy.
- Reactivation messaging (Section 6(d)) against an existing or dormant contact
  list requires Client's written attestation of consent **and** the
  regulated-segment sign-off above before Provider will run it.
