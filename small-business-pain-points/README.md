## The short version

- **Winner: LeadLoop** — an automated lead follow-up / missed-call text-back engine. Scores #1 on the rubric and is the fastest to revenue. Real-estate agents are the beachhead.
- **Build it on GoHighLevel (GHL) + WordPress now**, migrate to the AIBizConnect app later. This makes money while the app matures.
- **RedTape Radar** (compliance calendar) is the strongest second product; **PriceGuard** (margin calculator) ships as a free lead magnet.
- **Possible next product idea (not yet built):** an "FSBO Finder" tool that monitors Kijiji/Facebook Marketplace/Craigslist for GTA FSBO postings and AI-filters out disguised agent/wholesaler listings — see `2026-07-gta-realtor-leadgen-painpoints.md` §4a for the full viability writeup (real demand precedent, real ToS/scraping risk to scope first).
- **Separate thesis under validation (owner's own business, not a sellable vertical):** Al as the alt-ownership/distressed-inventory specialist agent — see `2026-07-alt-ownership-distressed-opportunity.md`. Recommended sequencing: buyer "program matcher" (Ourboro/rent-to-own referrals) first, investor "power-of-sale deal list" second; both reuse LeadLoop/GHL infrastructure rather than requiring new build.
